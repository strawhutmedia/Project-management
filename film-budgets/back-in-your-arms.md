## TL;DR — Can we make this for $700k?

**Yes, but only at the lower end of music licensing and with a scrappy
20-person crew.** Three scenarios:

| Scenario | Production | Post | Marketing | Admin | Direct | + Bond/Cont. (13%) | **TOTAL** | vs. $700k cap |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| **Lean** (festival music, 16 shoot days, deferred writer) | $462k | $73k | $50k | $30k | $615k | $80k | **$695k** | 🟢 fits ($5k margin) |
| **Mid** (broader music, 18 shoot days, paid writer) | $498k | $103k | $50k | $30k | $681k | $89k | **$770k** | 🔴 over by $70k |
| **Stretch** (full music clearance, 20 shoot days) | $545k | $148k | $50k | $35k | $778k | $101k | **$879k** | 🔴 over by $179k |

**Recommendation:** Aim for the **Lean** scenario. The single biggest
discretionary lever is music licensing. Festival-only rights at $10k vs. full
clearance at $75k is a $65k swing — bigger than any other line in the budget.

## Cost flags (scenes that hit harder than they look)

These are the ones to watch as we go scene-by-scene:

### 🚨 **XenoSouls clips** (scenes 7, 13, 16, 24, 60, 105, 109)
The script calls for actual sci-fi footage — Jack Robbins fighting "Bloodied
Man" slobbering on his face, throwing a grenade, sprinting from aliens. Three
ways to handle this:
- **(a) Shoot it ourselves** — half-day pickup with Jason in costume, one
  prosthetic make-up, one stunt actor, basement/garage location. ~$10k.
- **(b) Spoof footage** — clearly-fake terrible-prestige-TV vibe, almost
  comedy. Cheap, ~$3-5k. Risk: undercuts the joke that Sawyer is genuinely
  hooked.
- **(c) License existing genre footage** — usually impossible without major
  fees. Skip.

**Recommendation: (a) — shoot a stylized "XenoSouls montage" ourselves on a
dedicated half-day.** Counts as Scene 7-style insert across all the references.

### 🚨 **The mall sequence** (scenes 59–62)
Malls are the single hardest commercial location to permit and pay for. Two
options:
- **Real mall** with permit, security, off-hours shoot: ~$10-15k
- **Outlet center / dying retail strip / repurposed retail interior** (much
  cheaper, easier to control): ~$3-5k. The Sunglass Hut interior scene can be
  ANY storefront we control.
- The "DISTANT SHOTS" in the script style suggest handheld surveillance vibe —
  doesn't have to look like a real anchor mall.

**Recommendation: dying retail strip + a small storefront we redress as
"Sunglass Hut".** Saves $7-10k.

### 🚨 **The stab + hospital sequence** (scenes 134–141)
The knife-in-chest moment requires a practical SFX rig: prosthetic chest plate,
fixed knife, blood gag. Standard kit: ~$2-3k for the rig + SFX coordinator
day-rate. The hospital scenes (5 of them) are short and mostly close-ups; can
be shot at a decommissioned hospital wing or a clinic on a Sunday for $3-5k
total.

### 🚨 **Driving scenes** (~20 of them — INT/EXT car)
Big cost driver if done with a process trailer ($4-6k/day × 2 days = $8-12k).
Cheaper option: **poor-man's process** (parked car, lights swung past windows,
the scene is shot dialogue-first) for ~$1k per setup. Only the highway scenes
where we see the windshield and changing landscape need real driving plates.

**Recommendation:** poor-man's process for the talky in-car scenes (e.g. Sawyer
parked at Kendrick's house, Sawyer + Anna driving home from hospital), real
driving plates for ~3 specific scenes (Sawyer driving cross-country to
Solvang, the chase to the hospital after the stabbing).

### 🟡 **The LA fire reference** (Kendrick's monologue, scene 109)
Never shown on camera. Pure dialogue. **No money.** Just need Jason to deliver.

## Free location call: where does the ADU go?

You said the ADU is **one** of {Sawyer's Solvang house, Sawyer's Minneapolis
apartment, Justine & Tom's house}. My recommendation:

**Use the ADU as Sawyer's Solvang house.**

Why:
- **Solvang house has ~50 scenes** — the most of any single location in the
  film. Every dollar saved compounds.
- **Solvang house is geographically next to Jason Kendrick's house in the
  story** — having them physically next door (just walk from one to the
  other) makes movement between scenes free, no travel days needed.
- **Margo's "neighbor down the road"** intro at scene 42 plays better if
  Sawyer is in a similar dwelling — an ADU + main house IS a neighbor setup.
- The Minneapolis apartment is only 15 scenes and can be shot in a real
  apartment or even doubled with the new apartment at the end (155, 157, 160,
  161) by redressing.
- Justine & Tom's house is only 5 scenes — can be a friend's house for a
  long weekend.

**Net savings if ADU = Solvang house:** ~$10-15k vs. paid location for those
50 scenes.
