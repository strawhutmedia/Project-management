Jason Kendrick & Lilly Wright Throw in the Towel on Their Three-Year Marriage

The XenoSouls heartthrob and his actress wife are calling it quits — and a source says the cracks have been showing since his big-screen comeback went belly-up.

By Danni Vale, Senior Tea Correspondent Updated 2 hours ago  •  4 min sip
f𝕏🔗

Grab your mug, sippers, because this one is a scalder. Jason Kendrick — the brooding face of sci-fi juggernaut XenoSouls — and his wife of three years, actress Lilly Wright, are officially, capital-D done.

Reps for the couple confirmed the split late Tuesday in a joint statement so buttoned-up you could iron a shirt on it: “Jason and Lilly have made the difficult decision to part ways. They remain grateful for their time together and ask for privacy as they navigate this transition.” Translation, from us to you: something happened.

A source close to the pair tells TEA SIPPER the marriage has been running on fumes ever since Kendrick’s much-hyped return to the big screen face-planted at the box office last spring. “He took it hard. Harder than anybody outside the house knows,” the insider spills. “Jason’s been a different guy since XenoSouls wrapped. Lilly held the whole thing together with tape and a smile for as long as she could.”

“He hasn’t been himself. Nobody’s saying it out loud — but everybody sees it.” — A source close to the couple

The two met on the awards circuit, got engaged after a whirlwind five months, and married in an intimate Ojai ceremony that Wright once breathlessly called “the easiest yes of my life.” Easy come, easy go, we suppose.

Neither Kendrick nor Wright has posted since the news broke, though eagle-eyed sippers noticed Lilly quietly scrubbed her grid of nearly every photo of her soon-to-be-ex. Petty?
